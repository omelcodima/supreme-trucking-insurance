## Use

- Invite clients consistently after a real service interaction, regardless of how satisfied they are. Do not ask only people expected to give five stars.
- Do not offer discounts, gifts, or incentives, and do not write or submit reviews for clients.
- Use the QR on an office display or the direct link in a personal follow-up or email signature.
- This kit does not subscribe anyone to a mailing list or create automated email/text campaigns. Any texting workflow needs a separate consent and compliance review.

Google's guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122

## Website Source and Maintenance

Google Business Profile: https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11z72w_0z4&q=Supreme+Trucking+Insurance+Agency

The owner review panel displayed 5.0 from 2 reviews on September 6, 2026. Benjamin and Alex volkiv each had five stars. Display names were checked visually; excerpts were copied exactly from the reviews. Profile links are stored with the excerpts in `src/lib/googleReviews.ts`.

Only brief excerpts are reproduced. Author links and a link to all reviews lead back to Google. The visible checked date makes this a historical snapshot, not a live feed or a claim that Google verifies customer identity. No self-serving Review or AggregateRating structured data is added.

Before refreshing the snapshot, check the full current review list, update the rating/count/date together, and remove any excerpt no longer publicly available. Do not substitute generated testimonials. Review request wording remains neutral.

The static QR image is generated locally using `qrcode` 1.5.4, black on white with a four-module quiet zone. Decoding is checked at original, 144px and 288px sizes using `jsqr` 1.4.0. No QR service subscription or browser widget is needed.
